# OSCP Study Vault

![OSCP](https://img.shields.io/badge/OSCP-in%20progress-blue)

## About

I am a graduate student at the Georgia Institute of Technology pursuing my masters in Information Security. I am current pursuing the OSCP to further develop my technical skills in ethical hacking.  I am documenting my journey as a I study fir thus exam. 

## ⚠️ Scope & disclaimer

- These notes cover **lab / training machines only** (HTB, CPTS, PG).
- **No OSCP exam content** — the exam is under NDA and nothing about it appears here.
- For education and authorized testing only. Don't point these techniques at systems you don't own or have permission to test.
